# Upwork job hunter

Hourly Upwork job alerts on Telegram, filtered by your own rules (Best Match only, fresh posts, active clients with verified payment, good rating and spend, open to your country, at or above your rate, boost within your Connects budget). Runs as a Claude cloud routine, so no computer needs to stay on. Claude also drafts proposals on request, and never submits without your yes.

**Start here: [SETUP.md](SETUP.md).** The only file you edit by hand is `profile.md`, and setup fills it in for you.
